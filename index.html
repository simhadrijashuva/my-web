Methodology

How We Rank

Every title is scored on a weighted Bayesian mean — raw average pulled toward the global mean in proportion to how few votes it has. This stops obscure masterpieces from topping the chart on twelve votes, and stops blockbusters from riding hype. Scores are recalculated weekly.
